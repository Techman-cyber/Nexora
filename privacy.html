Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

What stays on your device

Your browsing history, bookmarks, downloads list, settings, profile choices (name, colour, avatar) and Power Centre data are stored locally in the browser on your computer. Nexora does not send them to Nexora servers.

If you sign in

Signing in with email, Google, GitHub or Yahoo uses Firebase Authentication (a Google service). Firebase receives the details needed to sign you in, such as your email address, name and a unique account ID. You can use Nexora as a guest without an account.

Services you choose to use

  • Search engines (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave, Ecosia) receive what you search for.
  • Search suggestions are requested from your chosen search engine while you type.
  • Translate page uses Google Translate on the page you choose.
  • Chrome Web Store and Google's extension servers are contacted when you install an extension.
  • Websites you visit can collect their own data under their own policies.

Ghost windows

Ghost windows do not save history, cookies or cache after they close.
